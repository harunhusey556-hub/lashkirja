# P2 features (feature/p2-features)

| # | Item | Result |
|---|------|--------|
| 1 | Spotlight index | Done. Core: `OpenTarget` (`lashkirja://customer/<id>`, `lashkirja://invoice/<id>`) and `SpotlightEntry` with tests (`SpotlightTests`). App: `SpotlightIndexer` (CoreSpotlight, on-device only), fed from the Asiakkaat and Myynti list loads via `AppModel.indexForSpotlight`; a tap arrives through `onContinueUserActivity` and `AppModel.handle(url:)`, which sets `pendingRoute` (Myynti tab, customer/invoice detail). `endLocalSession()` deletes the whole index. |
| 2 | Receipt thumbnails | Done. `ReceiptThumbnail` loads `/api/receipts/{id}/file/preview` (JPEG, 422 when no preview) through `DocumentCache` (keyed by `updatedAt`), downsampled off the main thread with ImageIO, in-memory NSCache; symbol while loading or failing. Only the Kuitit list rows (review queue and mail list unchanged). |
| 3 | Raportit export year | Skipped: `RaportitView` already sends `year` (the year switcher on screen) to `/api/export` and the footer names it. |
| 4 | Next invoice number | Done. `InvoiceSequence` (core, tested) reads `GET /api/invoices/sequence`; the new-invoice form shows "Seuraava laskunumero: N" as a caption under the title. Read-only, hidden on failure and when editing a draft. |

Known limits: Spotlight only adds or updates; a deleted or archived row stays findable until sign-out (a tap then shows the normal not-found state). Invoice indexing follows whatever list the owner has loaded (filters included).

Not touched: OTP, money math, Tap to Pay, passkeys, server schema.
